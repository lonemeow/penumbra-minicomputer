          Hello there!

This is the first ever demo for the Penumbra
architecture.
{pause 4}
What is Penumbra, you ask?
{pause 4}
It is my silly project to design a 32bit RISC
architecture from ground up and implement it
in FPGA (hopefully in discrete logic one day!)
{speed 0.2}...
{speed 4}
Penumbra is 2-operand, load-store, 16 register
architecture, with software-managed TLB.

{pause 10}
This demo is written for Penumbra 2.5 variant,
running on a Lattice ECP5 FPGA, with a CPU
core clock of 37.5MHz{speed 0.2}...
{speed 4}
This results in performance roughly similar to
an Intel 386DX at 33MHz.
{pause 20}
{clear}
During the entire demo, you can how the CPU is
performing at the bottom of the screen{speed 0.2}...

{speed 4}The number at the bottom left corner
shows the CPI (cycles per instruction) - when
everything is ideal, this CPU can reach 1 CPI{speed 0.2}...
{speed 4}
The colors indicate what is causing the CPU to
stall for some cycles reducing performance:

 {black}{on blue}Load from memory{pause 1}
 {on cyan}Store to memory{pause 1}
 {on red}Instruction fetch{pause 1}
 {on yellow}Complex instructions (div/mul){pause 1}
 {on ltred}Pipeline interlock or flush{pause 1}
{on black}{grey}{pause 8}
